### Significant qualifications found in this review

- Commonwealth non-government-school capital guidance generally restricts standalone equipment and pre-primary costs; the BGA needs the actual capital project. `cgp-guidelines`, `bga-contacts`.
- ACCAP Critical Infrastructure is **invitation-only**, despite a distant programme end date. The public overview was reviewed; full GO6332 GrantConnect documents were not retrievable, so no complete applicant/expenditure eligibility determination is encoded. Retirement villages are not presumed to be residential aged care. `accap`.
- Queensland schools themselves and their P&C/P&F legal applicants are different. Existing commitments and retirement-living/body-corporate improvement exclusions matter. Uncertain sponsored/government legal forms require confirmation, not an invented blanket yes/no decision. `qld-gcbf-eligibility`, `qld-gcbf-items`.
- NSW BGAS displays conflicting ongoing/open and application-closed wording. We do not resolve that in favour of an open application. BGA deadlines need direct confirmation. The BELP page's stale 'Open' presentation does not override its published 2025 deadline. `nsw-bgas`, `nsw-belp`.
- NSW Inclusive Environments **child-based** applications are distinct from service-based/readiness funding. The scope is the eligible preschool and specific adjustment, not any childcare electronics purchase. Commitment-before-application rules must not be casually rewritten as commitment-before-approval. `nsw-ie`.
- Victorian hearing support is a targeted adjustment with audiologist evidence, portability and personal-device compatibility requirements, not a fixed campus PA replacement fund. Closed school/Building Blocks rounds are not advertised as available grants. `vic-hearing`, `vic-abp`, relevant building-fund sources.
- WA Direct to Market is school-funded procurement, **not new money**. Lotterywest needs the eligible applicant and community outcome; non-standard legal forms/auspices need confirmation, and unincorporated not-for-profits are not automatically excluded simply because they are unincorporated. `wa-supply`, `wa-lotterywest`.
- SA Flying Start concerns additional eligible preschool capacity and funded-service commitments, including documented delivery plans. Its usual co-investment terms are not a blanket percentage back on a PA quote. The application/commitment sequence requires scrutiny. `sa-flying`, `sa-flying-guidelines`.
- SA Julia Farr has substantial applicant, routine phone/IT/core-cost, gaming and project-duplication exclusions. Educational institutions cannot apply; a separately constituted community applicant at an education site needs confirmation rather than an automatic approval or a site-name-only legal judgment. `sa-jfdi`, `sa-jfdi-guidelines`.
