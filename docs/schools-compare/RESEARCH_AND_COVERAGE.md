# Phase 4 — Australian schools comparison research and coverage
Review date: 25 September 2026. Version: au-schools-comparison-2026-09-25-v1.

## Basis and limits
The live SmartComms NZ schools comparison (https://smartcomms.co.nz/compare/schools) was used for the user experience: job-led shortlists, capability comparison, platform profiles, scope/cost discussion, examples and buying questions. No NZ provider, installed price, testimonial or supply claim was reused as Australian evidence.

The primary-publication review supports the selected product descriptions and Australian reference routes. It is not a procurement census, physical product test, site survey, current-stock check, reference-customer interview or warranty verification. Shortlists and value commentary are attributed to SiteComms editorial judgement, not independent laboratory ratings. No live supplier contact took place.

## Main conclusions
- SPON has substantial manufacturer documentation for integrated school communication and scalable central management. It is now a main option rather than a conditional appendix. No verified Australian distributor/support route or named Australian school deployment was established in this review. Require that evidence before making a purchase recommendation.
- FrontRow's classroom/campus combination and published local provider route deserve their own positive treatment.
- Algo's 8188 documents talkback. It is inaccurate to treat the whole range as one-way just because the 8301 is a paging adapter.
- Axis C1710 documents audio, text, strobe and two-way functionality. Its capabilities depend on the management configuration. Do not inherit an outdated “no visual display” assessment.
- Bodet's bells/time/visual proposition is not the same as a full room-intercom package.
- PROSPERO's Australian-facing PA documentation is not evidence of classroom intercom or Australian voice-alarm certification.
- TOA family selection matters: IP-A1PG is not itself a 100V amplifier.
- Australian Monitor's school design includes discontinued equipment; it illustrates architecture, not a current bill of materials.
- No like-for-like Australian installed-price sample supports a numerical cheapest/best-value ordering. The existing SiteComms calculator is not a brand quote.

## Australian geographical coverage
QLD: provider-published Macrosphere bell/PA services (search extract, direct retrieval timed out); separate SCP Audio Australia hall example.
NSW: ClearaSound's Conductor service and stated NSW/QLD coverage.
VIC: Vision One's St Carlo Borromeo Primary School assembly/performance audio project; explicitly not a whole-campus IP bell or SPON case.
WA: advanceNET's Perth contact route plus relevant product listings, not a guaranteed onsite installer.
SA: Teach TEK school bell/PA service description and Adelaide location, not a named brand deployment.
TAS/ACT/NT: the national technical comparison applies; equivalent local deployment evidence was not established in this iteration. This is stated on the page.

## Excluded or qualified evidence
- An unfinished Visionext school-case article contains placeholder/example bullets; it was not relied on as a robust named, brand-specific school deployment.
- An Aloysius College mention did not establish the specific campus/state/system required to call it a South Australian Conductor installation.
- The previously mentioned Jordan Springs/Q-SYS case was not independently verified here; it is not published on this page.
- Q-SYS is a broader programmable AV/control signpost, not a verified packaged bell/intercom system.
- AtlasIED's Australian distribution announcement is historical evidence; current appointment/model availability must be reconfirmed.
- Australian SCP Audio's hall case is not evidence for SPON. Do not confuse similarly named businesses or overseas reseller listings.
- ITC remains outside the main shortlist because the relevant Australian supply/support evidence was not established, not because absence was proven.
- Procurement or directory presence does not establish official endorsement, approved-contractor status, every-state installation capability or a SiteComms partnership.

## Source governance
The authoritative page data is `src/lib/content/school-compare.ts`. Profiles, shortlists, matrix and the public source register use these records; `docs/comparison-research.json` is a checked snapshot, not a separate authority.
`reviewed_at` means publication/evidence review. It does not mean stock, warranty, performance or commercial verification. No `verified_at` is manufactured. The Macrosphere record expressly records search-extract-only access.
Owners: T3 Labs / SiteComms editorial owner, with owner approval required for commercial representations and launch. A named maintenance owner/cadence should be set before launch.
Recheck withdrawn products, licence/management changes, supplier appointments and material claims before purchase recommendations. Change the affected source record, dependent profile, snapshot and tests together; do not reset every source date on a site build.
No new automated crawler, external write, server-side product selector or AI protocol is introduced.
