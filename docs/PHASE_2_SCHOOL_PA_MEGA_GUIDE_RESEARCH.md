# Phase 2 - School PA Systems Australia Mega Guide

Date: 2026-10-20
Canonical page: `/schools`

## Why this is the first mega page

The Search Console export supplied for this audit is still young, but it already shows the broad school-PA cluster beginning to form:

- `pa systems for schools`: 12 impressions, average position 25.67
- `pa system for schools`: 2 impressions, average position 21.00
- `/schools`: 18 impressions, average position 21.67
- `/compare/schools`: 59 impressions, 3 clicks, average position 15.34

The school-bell cluster is already being addressed on `/systems/school-bell-announcements`. Creating a separate new `school-pa-systems-australia` URL would risk splitting the broader school intent between two pages, so the existing `/schools` canonical has been rebuilt as the broad buyer guide instead.

## Editorial model

The page does not copy or combine competitor wording. It extracts the buyer questions and useful distinctions visible across multiple current Australian sources, verifies the factual basis, and writes an independent decision framework around them.

Provider and manufacturer sources are treated as evidence of:

- product/service approaches they publicly describe;
- workflows or implementation examples they publish;
- Australian projects they attribute to themselves.

They are **not** treated as independent proof of performance, universal suitability, market share, SiteComms partnership or whole-of-Australia installation coverage.

Government sources are treated as jurisdiction-specific requirements/benchmarks and are not generalised into a national rule.

## Government / authority references

### Victoria - Building Quality Standards Handbook, technical specifications
Source: https://www.schoolbuildings.vic.gov.au/building-quality-standards-handbook/technical-specifications

Current public-address requirements include, among other items:

- coverage across facilities and the entire site, including outdoor areas and future modular locations;
- routine, situational, important and emergency announcements;
- simple/logical operation for staff;
- school bell services;
- automatic announcement capability;
- simple/efficient zone selection;
- engagement with the department ICT team where the PA requires school-supplied network connectivity.

These are Victorian government-school project requirements, not a national specification.

### NSW - EFSG 2026, Part D Design and Technical Requirements
Current landing page: https://education.nsw.gov.au/about-us/efsg-home/efsg-2026/design-and-technical-requirements

The current EFSG 2026 is applicable to projects whose business case is approved on or after 8 September 2026. The current Part D includes requirements for new/refurbished communications and says existing reticulated public-address and period-bell services should be assessed; where in use, functionality is to be retained or enhanced, with upgrade/replacement/relocation/extension considered as part of project works.

The PDF itself identifies some sections as content unchanged from EFSG 2025; the page therefore links to the current 2026 Part D rather than pretending every paragraph was newly authored in 2026.

## Gap identified in the current Australian SERP

Most ranking provider pages answer only part of the buyer problem:

- coverage/features but little architecture comparison;
- a network solution without the dedicated-wired counterargument;
- a dedicated-wired solution without the IP/hybrid case;
- a bell timer without explaining when a wider platform is justified;
- product capability without neutral procurement/requirements context;
- good project evidence but no independent price/funding path.

The SiteComms page can therefore be broader without being derivative by combining:

1. direct answer to what a school PA system must achieve;
2. four architecture choices and their trade-offs;
3. retain-vs-replace decision;
4. day-to-day staff workflows;
5. current Australian government examples;
6. eight attributed provider/manufacturer approaches;
7. network-vs-dedicated decision questions;
8. the existing SiteComms pricing model;
9. a project brief/specification pathway;
10. funding and independent SiteComms next-step help.

## Conversion path

The mega guide does not turn every section into a sales CTA. The natural progression is:

- broad research -> compare architectures/systems;
- budget intent -> pricing tool;
- procurement intent -> specification checklist;
- funding intent -> funding checker;
- decision-stage uncertainty -> SiteComms project review.

The SiteComms enquiry wording preserves the confidentiality promise:

- SiteComms reviews the request itself;
- SiteComms does not automatically forward the enquiry;
- contact details, attachments and tool answers are not sent to installers/suppliers;
- SiteComms may reply with public provider contacts and its reasoning;
- the user decides whether to contact anyone suggested.

## AI/search readability choices

The page is structured for extraction without manufacturing FAQ/schema spam:

- query-aligned H1 and metadata;
- direct answer near the top;
- descriptive H2 questions;
- an explicit architecture comparison table;
- short factual provider-source summaries with outbound links;
- jurisdiction caveats next to government requirements;
- concise FAQs answering common buyer questions;
- Article schema with genuine reviewer metadata;
- no fabricated ratings, market share, partnerships or “best” claims.
